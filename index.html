Northgate Branch Library

The Northgate branch is open Monday to Friday from 9am to 6pm, and on Saturday from 10am to 4pm. The branch is closed on Sundays and on public holidays.

Borrowing is free for all residents. Members may hold up to twelve items at a time, with a standard loan period of three weeks. Renewals can be made in person or by telephone.

The reading room on the first floor seats forty and is available without booking. The two study rooms at the rear may be reserved up to a fortnight in advance.
